## 2. Accounts, workspaces, and team access

A platform account is separate from a workspace. A user may own no more than one workspace and may hold memberships in other workspaces. Workspace creation is an explicit action. Workspace owners control workspace configuration and invite team members using workspace-specific roles.

You must provide accurate information required to use the Service and keep access credentials secure. Do not share your authenticated session or invite another person to use your account. Workspace owners are responsible for assigning appropriate roles and promptly revoking access that is no longer required. Moderators and other members may act only within the permissions granted to them.

Twitch's native channel moderator status does not, by itself, grant platform workspace access. A workspace invitation must be accepted by the intended Twitch identity.

The Service may connect to Twitch, Spotify, YouTube/Invidious instances, and optional Lyrion-compatible systems. Each third-party service is operated independently and is subject to its own terms, privacy practices, availability, and authorization model. You authorize the Service to make only the provider requests required for the features you enable.

You must comply with the applicable third-party terms and policies. The Service may disable an integration or capability when the provider, authorization scope, technical conditions, contractual terms, or rights-use review do not support it.

It is separate from the platform's broadcast playback route. Spotify content must not be treated as an audio source for the Twitch/OBS broadcast path. Optional Lyrion use requires you to have rights to the media in the connected library and authorization to control the connected server/player.

## 6. Public overlays and access links

Overlay scene URLs are read-only access links intended for OBS browser sources. Anyone who obtains a valid scene URL may be able to view the information configured for that scene. Do not publish or share a scene URL unless you intend its content to be accessible to the recipients. Protect URLs as bearer credentials, keep them out of public logs and unrelated messages, and revoke/replace a URL promptly if it is exposed.

Workspace owners are responsible for reviewing which request, queue, announcement, artwork, and other information appears in each published scene. The Operator may revoke a scene link where required for security, legal compliance, or enforcement.
